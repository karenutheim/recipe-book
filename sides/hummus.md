# Hummus

## Ingredients

- 2 cans of chick peas
- juice of 0,5 lemon
- 4 cloves of garlic
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp tahini
- 0,5 tsp ground cumin
- 1,25 dl neutral oil
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley



## Instructions

- Mix all ingredients except parsley in a blender until homogenous
- Add parsley on top and serve
